# Project: crypto-exchange-np

1. **Product:** Bitkosh ("bit" + kosh, a digital treasury), a crypto exchange for Nepal, built ahead of legalisation. Spot and perpetual futures, with NPR withdrawals straight to Nepali bank accounts (no P2P). Users: Nepali retail and institutional traders, global institutions, global market makers.
2. **Stack:** Rust for matching, risk and liquidation, the ledger, and REST/WebSocket/FIX gateways; C++ for the market-maker gateway; Next.js for web and admin; Expo for iOS and Android; shared TypeScript packages; Postgres, Redis and Redpanda (Kafka API); Solidity and other chain integrations for custody only.
3. **Layout:** monorepo with `engine/`, `services/`, `mm-gateway/`, `web/`, `mobile/`, `packages/`, `contracts/`, `infra/`.
4. **Stage and goal:** greenfield, before legalisation. "Good" means launch-ready, audited and regulator-presentable: spot and perp on test funds, NPR deposits and withdrawals end to end against a simulated bank, institutions and market makers connected, clean security and reconciliation reports.
5. **Environments:** local; dev (deploys automatically from `main`); public testnet (test funds, same API as prod, blockchain testnets, simulated bank); prod.
6. **Tests:** every change has tests; invariant checks and deterministic replay for the ledger and matching engine; fuzzed gateways and parsers.
7. **Security and compliance:** no secrets in code; `cargo deny`, `pnpm audit` and secret scanning; a threat model for custody and withdrawals; multi-person approval for large withdrawals; external and smart-contract audits before launch. KYC, AML and sanctions hooks; audit logs that cannot be edited; minimal personal data. Nepal-specific rules to be added later.
8. **Performance targets:** matching engine p99 under 1 ms, API order round trip p99 under 20 ms in-region, market data under 50 ms. Trading available 99.9%. Zero ledger mismatches: reconcile daily and pause withdrawals automatically on a mismatch.
9. **Workflow:** a solo developer working with AI agents (Claude Code, and Grok in Cursor for some tasks). Sprints on a board (Notion or Linear; until one exists, `tasks/todo.md`). One task per `feat/<ticket>-name` branch off `origin/main` (`.claude/commands/build.md` has the cloud-session rule), Conventional Commits, small squash-merged PRs. Each session starts with `/pickup` and ends with `/learn` (when there is a lesson) and `/handoff`.
10. **Merge gate:** CI green (`rust-lint`, `rust-test`, `integration`, `ts`, `build` and `security` jobs), then `/tiered-review`: stage 1 by senior-engineer and the module owners, stage 2 by lead-engineer. The full gate (every owner the diff touches) runs on specs, ADRs, plans and money code; other PRs run senior-engineer, the task's named owners and lead-engineer (tiers approved 2026-10-30). The verdicts and the user's approval, quoted word for word, are posted as PR comments. Then the user merges, or Claude merges only when the user says so in that session.
11. **Deploys:** dev on every merge to `main`, testnet from a release tag, prod from the same tag after manual approval, with a rollback plan.
12. **What to avoid:** generic skills that assume the wrong stack or tools and do not think from this project's perspective.
13. **Current milestone:** Milestone 1 is spot only, for Nepali retail users on an invite-only testnet with test funds and synthetic identities (no real identity documents before mainnet), on Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana and Tron. Perpetuals, institutions and market makers come in Milestone 2. `SPEC.md` is the source of truth for scope and decisions, and wins over this summary when they differ.
